# TENNIS PrizePicks: 2026-11-07
11 lines priced across 6 games; not priced: 1. Lists per kickoff day. Each line is priced from a point-by-point simulation of the match. Serve and return ratings are solved jointly with every opponent's, so 70% won against weak returners counts for less (TennisMyLife match database: ATP tour and Challengers, WTA tour; WTA 125s from the WTA's own feed; Tennis Abstract when a player isn't covered), with day-to-day form measured from real matches. ATP total-games and games-won lines move the simulation's winner to the market's price (Tennis Explorer) when the match is listed. Walk-forward test, Sep-Nov 2026 (ATP 1,723 / WTA 350 matches): winner log loss ATP 0.669 -> 0.640 with the ratings, 0.608 with the market; ATP games-won lines 0.686 -> 0.673; total games stayed near a coin flip for every version, so a total without Dabble's price stays Low. Elo, fatigue, indoor/outdoor and handedness didn't improve the test and aren't used; nor are hit rates or head-to-head (L30). No locks.

**No leg clears the ENTRY rule (High, 65%+). Sitting out beats forcing it.**
