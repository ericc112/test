9 lines priced across 5 games; not priced: 1. Lists per kickoff day. Each line is priced from a point-by-point simulation of the match. Serve and return ratings are solved jointly with every opponent's, so 70% won against weak returners counts for less (TennisMyLife match database: ATP tour and Challengers, WTA tour; WTA 125s from the WTA's own feed; Tennis Abstract when a player isn't covered), with day-to-day form measured from real matches. ATP total-games and games-won lines move the simulation's winner to the market's price (Tennis Explorer) when the match is listed. Elo, fatigue, indoor/outdoor and handedness didn't improve the test and aren't used; nor are hit rates or head-to-head (L30). No locks.
