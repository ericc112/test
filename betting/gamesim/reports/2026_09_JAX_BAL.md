## 2. Engine

Every snap, with down, distance, spot, clock and timeouts. Fitted on 47,664 scrimmage plays and 5,511 fourth downs before the game: run or pass call, 4th-down go/punt/FG, pass result (complete, incomplete, INT, sack), yards from real plays of the same down group, distance and spot (inside the 20 as a share of the distance to goal), penalties (6.6% of snaps), seconds to the next snap by result and tempo, timeouts, the two-minute warning, punts, kickoffs, onside kicks (recovered 8% of 64), FGs by distance and wind, XP 95.4%, two-point 49.2%. ## 4. Market comparison and decision rule

Rule: PLAY only if the edge over the no-vig price is at least 2 points **and** the edge minus 1.5 × total error is above zero.

| Bet | Model | No-vig market | Edge | Total error | Edge − 1.5 × error | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BAL moneyline | 46.9% | 20.4% | +26.5 pts | ±11.4 pts | +9.5 pts | **PLAY** |
| JAX moneyline | 53.1% | 79.6% | -26.5 pts | ±11.4 pts | -43.5 pts | **PASS** |

| JAX -9.5 | 22.4% | 49.6% | -27.1 pts | ±11.6 pts | -44.6 pts | **PASS** |
| Over 42.5 | 64.1% | 48.9% | +15.2 pts | ±11.9 pts | -2.6 pts | **PASS** |
| Under 42.5 | 35.9% | 51.1% | -15.2 pts | ±11.9 pts | -33.0 pts | **PASS** |

Total error = √(MC² + structural² + starter spread²). Structural = out-of-sample RMS gap to the closing no-vig market in this season's backtest (win 10.5%, cover 11.1%, total 11.5%). Starter spread = range across the three depth-chart QBs ÷ 4 (win 4.2%).
